## Write with the world beside you

Writing Studio is deliberately quieter than the rest of the archive. The manuscript remains primary; world context appears when it is useful and stays out of the way when it is not.

## Two kinds of document

Manuscripts hold stories, essays, and other long-form work. Reference pages hold research, setting notes, plot ideas, and supporting material. Both can link back to the shared world model.

## Archive first

Daena remains a world archive first. Writing belongs here because stories and worlds inform each other, not because the studio is trying to replace every dedicated writing tool.
