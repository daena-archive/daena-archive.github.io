## Give history somewhere to happen

Events can be brief markers or detailed archive entries. Dates provide order without becoming the only way to understand a moment: participants, locations, relationships, and prose remain part of the same record.

## Calendars on your terms

Use familiar dates when they fit. When the world needs its own structure, define years, months, weeks, seasons, and eras. Daena keeps authored values literal rather than silently filling in detail you did not provide.

## Project history is separate

Timeline is for the history inside the world. Git snapshots and recovery tools describe the history of the project itself.
