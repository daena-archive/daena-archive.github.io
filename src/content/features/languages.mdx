## Start with a name, go deeper when needed

A language can begin as a page of notes and a handful of terms. Vocabulary, sounds, writing, grammar, and examples are there when the language demands more precision.

## Culture stays in view

Languages share the same archive as people, places, and cultures. Vocabulary can grow from the world instead of becoming a disconnected linguistic database.

## Current boundaries

Language is part of Daena's active beta. Vocabulary, sounds, writing, grammar, and examples are already covered, while guides and editing help continue to mature.
