## Every world needs somewhere to exist

Maps are not isolated attachments. Locations, events, people, and map roles stay attached to the same shared entities used elsewhere in Daena.

## Replaceable map systems

Imported source files stay as they are, while names, roles, and links remain in your archive. That keeps provider integrations replaceable and prevents one source from defining the structure of an entire project.

## Beta, by design

The Maps workspace already supports physical-world creation and OpenLayers vector editing with image import, but terrain detail, editing, and provider integration continue to develop. The roadmap labels future layers explicitly.
