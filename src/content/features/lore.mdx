## What it lets you do

Start with an article when all you need is a place to think. Add structure when a detail needs to be compared, searched, or connected elsewhere. A record can remain simple or grow into a full reference entry without being moved into another system.

## One identity across the archive

Daena does not create a different version of Captain Elian Rook for the family tree, the map, the timeline, and the manuscript. Houses, Maps, Timeline, and Writing each point back to the same person or place. That keeps names, links, assets, and context close even as the world grows.

## Current boundaries

Lore is part of Daena's active beta. Its core record and relationship workflow is usable, while schemas and interaction details may still evolve before beta.
