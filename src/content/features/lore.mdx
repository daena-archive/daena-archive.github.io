## What it lets you do

Start with an article when all you need is a place to think. Add structure when a detail needs to be compared, searched, or connected elsewhere. A record can remain simple or grow into a full reference entry without being moved into another system.

## One identity across the archive

Daena does not create a different version of Captain Elian Rook for the map, timeline, and manuscript. Each surface refers back to the same stable entity. That keeps names, links, assets, and context close even as the world grows.

## Current boundaries

Lore is part of Daena's active alpha. Its core record and relationship workflow is usable, while schemas and interaction details may still evolve before beta.
