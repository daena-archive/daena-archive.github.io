---
title: Creating entries
description: Build a lore record from prose, fields, relationships, and assets.
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

Choose a contributed template—such as Person, Place, Artifact, Faction, Culture, or Concept—then give the record a name. Write freely in its document and add structured fields only where they provide lasting value.

Use relationships to connect context, and attach assets to the owning entity rather than scattering path references through prose.
