Dates may use familiar Gregorian structure or a calendar defined by the project — with your own years, months, weeks, seasons, and eras. Fictional years such as `1` and `42` remain literal authored values, not conversions to a real-world calendar.

Time is optional. Daena extends a date only when you provide time detail; it does not invent missing components to make a value look complete.

## Calendars and eras

- **Create a calendar** when your world needs its own reckoning; otherwise keep dates simple with the familiar structure.
- **Link eras in Details → Chronology.** Era membership lives there rather than in a separate date field, so the chronology stays consistent when dates change.
- **Reuse across records.** Calendars are project records of their own (a Timeline template). Eras adopt a calendar directly; events and encounters reach it through their era link, so sharing an era means sharing its reckoning.

Calendar creation uses the same lifecycle language as other records — New, Edit identity, Archive — and the same revision safeguards. If two views edit the same date, keep your draft and choose Reload current values, Review draft, or Reapply draft onto current rather than overwriting.
