---
title: Calendars and dates
description: Use Gregorian or authored fictional calendar systems.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

Dates may use familiar Gregorian structure or a calendar defined by the project. Fictional years such as `1` and `42` remain literal authored values.

Time is optional. Daena extends a date only when you provide time detail; it does not invent missing components to make a value look complete.
